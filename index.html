I am a PhD candidate in economics at the Paris School of Economics and EHESS, supervised by Ekaterina Zhuravskaya. I am on the job market in 2026–27.

I work on the political economy of media. My research asks how digital media changes what people read, believe and do — and what that means for politics. I use quasi-experimental methods on large-scale administrative and commercial panel data. I also work in education and health economics.
